# Say what is unclear about a time

A blank time may mean no time was supplied. It may also mean two readings are possible or the sources disagree.

The program stores the value separately from the reason for uncertainty. It can keep a known day without inventing an exact hour.

The planned appointment time, actual completion time, and source status-update time are different facts. One cannot silently replace another.
