# Two likely mistakes that need human review

The records contain strong clues about two mistakes. The program keeps the originals and proposes corrections. A clue is not permission to change a fact.

## A possible wrong year

One Lakewood Air job says it is done. Its scheduled date is November 10, 2026, after the demo's "today."

But its creation and status records are from 2025. Nearby job records also point to November 2025.

That supports a proposal to change both the start and end years to 2025. It does not prove the exact date the work finished.

Until a person approves the correction, the date stays uncertain. Simply seeing two different years is not enough. A December job can legitimately finish in January.

## A possible swapped house number

One spreadsheet row says `5832 Belmont Ave`. Other records support `5823 Belmont Ave` for the same contact.

The last two digits are swapped. The other records make `5823` a strong candidate, but one contact can have several homes.

The program must not silently move that work to `5823`. The service and date also differ from another spreadsheet row, so the two jobs are not automatically duplicates.

These checks support two proposed corrections. They do not establish that every hidden mistake in the dataset has been found.
