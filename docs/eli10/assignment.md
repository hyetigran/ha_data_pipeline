# What the assignment asks for

Build a program that combines messy home-service records and lets an AI assistant ask questions about them.

The supplied files cover six source types: three business systems, a spreadsheet, calendars, and provider profiles. A street file helps locate homes. Use November 2, 2026 as today.

The program must answer these questions:

1. What work happened near 4512 Live Oak St in the last two years?
2. Which handymen have worked in ZIP 75214 and have two free hours in the next three days?
3. What visits are planned on the 4500 block of Live Oak St in the next two weeks?
4. What do we know about Lone Star Plumbing, including its next three openings?
5. What work happened at 5823 Belmont Ave?
6. Which plumber fits a water-heater replacement near ZIP 75206 this week, and why?

The program must keep the source of each fact, handle Dallas time correctly, and hide homeowner contact details by default. Its MCP tools must not change source data.

The required hand-in has four parts:

- Code, a setup command, and tests.
- A one-to-two-page explanation of the data model, matching, openings, and reasons for the choices.
- A video under five minutes with all six answers and one weak or wrong answer explained.
- A decision log: choices, things left out, likely scale problems, and next steps.

The grading weights are data model 25%, matching 20%, MCP tools 20%, availability 15%, privacy and evidence 10%, and communication 10%.

Booking, payment, messaging, and real CRM connections are outside the assignment. Optional extras come after the required work. The original deadline was two days.
