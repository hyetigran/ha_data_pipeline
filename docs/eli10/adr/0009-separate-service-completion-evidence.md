# Store proof of finished work separately

A job's overall status does not describe every finished visit.

A completion record says what evidence shows that work happened. It belongs to a job and, when supported, a particular visit.

Several sources supporting one completion must not create several finished visits. A completed job also does not prove every listed service was done.

Trade groups can overlap. Adding all group counts may count the same job more than once.
