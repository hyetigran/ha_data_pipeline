# Keep the original claim and the chosen fact

One source may give a different address or date from another. Picking a shared value must not erase that disagreement.

The program keeps the original record, a cleaned source record, and the shared record used for answers.

A job can remain in the data even when its home is unresolved. The program does not invent a home just to fill the blank. Exact-home answers must disclose the resulting gaps.
