## Verified November 1 case

The clock falls back at 02:00 daylight time. The raw HCP start `2026-11-01T01:30:00` can represent either **06:30 UTC (CDT)** or **07:30 UTC (CST)**. Its 03:00 end is unambiguous at 09:00 UTC. The elapsed duration could therefore be 2.5 hours or 1.5 hours. We do not choose one.

The record's `started_at=01:22` and `on_my_way_at=01:05` are also within the repeated hour. They are retained raw and do not settle the ambiguity. `completed_at=02:45` is unambiguous at 08:45 UTC and still supports completed history. "Unknown appointment start" does not mean "unknown whether completed."

The new test reads this exact raw row, verifies both UTC possibilities independently through `fold=0/1`, requires `instant()` to reject the unzoned start, checks the adapter's raw retention and issues, and checks end/completion conversion. Six synthetic probes separately cover before/after transition and explicit offsets. One isolated recurrence-library test uses the actual Abrams calendar to verify wall-clock preservation. That test does not add recurrence expansion to the production pipeline.
